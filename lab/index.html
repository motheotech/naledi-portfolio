Try it

Four simulations built from the work I do every week. They run entirely in your browser on fictional data, and none of them reach a real system.

Scenario text is in English, the working language of the service desk. Buttons and results follow your language choice.

Ticket triage

Set a priority, choose a first action. The clock runs while you decide. There is no score for speed: the point is whether the call was right.

Six tickets based on the patterns that come up most: access, executives, new starters, telephony, phishing and the slow printer.

Admin console

A simulated shell against a fictional tenant. Type help to start. Arrow keys walk your history and Tab completes a command.

Try ticket-stats, m365-status, users or assets. lang st switches the whole site to Sesotho.

naledi@motheo: admin console

New starter and leaver

Pick the next step from the list. There is more than one right order, but some steps depend on others.

Switch between a new starter and a leaver at the top. Jumping ahead is allowed; the warning explains what it would break.

Network fault-finding

A computer lab has no internet. Every test costs time. Find the fault in as few tests as you can, then apply the right fix.

One of four faults is picked at random. Nodes turn green or amber as tests prove them. New fault gives you a different one.

How I work

Most escalations I inherit were not hard problems. Nobody confirmed the scope, and nobody told the user anything for two days.

  • Establish scope first

    One user or forty? Service Health before settings. The answer decides whether this is a fix or an announcement.

  • Restore, then resolve

    A workaround that gets someone into their meeting counts. Root cause can wait until the business is running again.

  • Leave a record

    Every action goes in the ticket in plain language. Asset records too: if the register and the shelf disagree, it goes in writing.
